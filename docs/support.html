Guides: Free photo cleaner, no subscription · Delete similar photos on iPhone

My iPhone storage is still full after deleting photos

Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted and stay there for 30 days, so you can recover them. They still take up space until Recently Deleted is emptied. iOS does that by itself after 30 days; to get the space back now:

  1. Open the Photos app.
  2. Scroll down to Utilities and tap Recently Deleted (unlock it with Face ID or your passcode).
  3. Tap Select, then Delete All, and confirm.

Apps can't empty Recently Deleted for you; only you can, in Photos. Settings › General › iPhone Storage can take a few minutes to show the new total.

How do I delete all screenshots on my iPhone?

  1. In Unhoard, open Screenshots & Recordings. It lists every screenshot and screen recording, oldest first, with their total size.
  2. Tap Select All. Favourites, edited screenshots and ones you put in your own albums are skipped; tap them if you want them too.
  3. Tap the Delete button, check the tray, and delete. iOS asks you to confirm once.
  4. Empty Recently Deleted (see above) to get the space back.

Prefer to go one by one? Use Swipe: left to delete, right to keep.

I use an iCloud Shared Library

Deleting an item that is in an iCloud Shared Library removes it for everyone in that library, not just you. It goes to their Recently Deleted too. If you share a library, check the delete tray carefully before you delete, or ask the others first.

How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

iOS can show you which servers each app contacts, with App Privacy Report:

  1. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › App Privacy Report, and turn it on.
  2. Use Unhoard as usual: scan, review and delete.
  3. Go back to App Privacy Report and look under App Network Activity.

Unhoard contacts no servers, so it shouldn't appear there. The only exception is if you asked Claude for a second opinion: then you'll see api.anthropic.com, and nothing else. You can also read the source code.

Does Unhoard find duplicates?

It finds similar shots: photos taken within two minutes of each other that look alike, with the best one of each group marked. For exact duplicates, use Apple's own tool: Photos › Utilities › Duplicates, which merges them safely. More in Delete similar photos on iPhone.

Why isn't everything selected for me?

On purpose. Only Similar Shots selects photos for you (and it keeps the best of each group). Favourites, edited photos and photos in your own albums are never selected for you, and Sensitive photos are never selected in bulk. You decide everything else.

Blurry only found a few photos

That's normal. Truly blurry photos are rare in most libraries, and Unhoard leaves out dark and night shots, because their noise can't be told apart from detail. Detection isn't perfect, so nothing in Blurry is selected for you.

Do I need Claude or an API key?

No. Unhoard finds and cleans everything without it. The second opinion is an optional extra for borderline photos. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iOS 27) it can run on the iPhone. To use Claude instead, tap Second Opinion Settings on the home screen and paste your own key from console.anthropic.com. Requests are billed to your Anthropic account. Before anything is sent you see the exact small previews that will go; see the privacy policy.

The scan stopped when I left the app

When you leave, the scan keeps going for as long as iOS allows. It's saved every few seconds and picks up where it left off next time you open Unhoard. If a scan is unfinished, iOS can also finish it overnight while your iPhone is charging.

I deleted something by mistake

Open Photos › Utilities › Recently Deleted, select it and tap Recover. Items stay there for 30 days unless you empty it.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. Unhoard is open source under the MIT license.
