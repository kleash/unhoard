Free photo cleaner for iPhone, no subscription

You can clean up a lot without installing anything. Start with what's built in, then use an app for the rest.

Free, built into your iPhone

Duplicates

Photos › scroll to Utilities › Duplicates. Photos finds copies of the same picture and lets you Merge them, keeping one.

Screenshots and screen recordings

Photos › scroll to Media Types › Screenshots (or Screen Recordings). Tap Select, drag your finger across the rows to select many at once, then tap the bin.

Big videos and other suggestions

Settings › General › iPhone Storage shows what's using space, and may suggest things to review, such as large videos. Tap Photos in the list for its details.

Get the space back

Everything you delete waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To free up space now: Photos › Utilities › Recently Deleted › Select › Delete All. No app can do this for you.

Where an app helps

Photos has no view for blurry photos, receipts and other utility shots, accidental pocket clips, near-identical re-takes or media saved by chat apps, and selecting a few thousand items by hand is slow. That's what photo cleaner apps add.

Many of them are free to download but ask for a subscription before you can delete much. Read what's free before you start.

Unhoard: one free option

Unhoard is free, with no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. Its code is open source under the MIT license.

  • Finds screenshots and recordings, similar shots, blurry photos, utility shots, large videos, accidental clips, short clips and media saved from apps.
  • Swipe through them or select them in a grid, then delete everything in one step.
  • Never selects your favourites, edited photos or photos in your albums for you.
  • Scans on your iPhone; the developer collects no data.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't find exact duplicates (use the Duplicates album above), compress videos or back up your photos.

The one thing that can cost money is optional: if you ask Claude for a second opinion on borderline photos, you use your own Anthropic API key, and Anthropic bills you for it. You don't need it to clean anything.
